# Publish Code or Data

If you have project datasets or code to share with the MSD-LIVE community or the general public, you can upload them to the <a href="https://data.msdlive.org/uploads/new" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**Data Repository**</a>.

Our upload form provides a rich set of metadata to help others find your data. It also gives you the opportunity to mint a DOI for your data if desired.
In order to upload data, you must belong to at least one project and the dataset must be directly funded by and relevant to that project.
When uploading your data, you must select one or more projects to associate your submission with, and one project is also assigned the special role of "Reviewing Project".

A curator from the Reviewing Project will verify that your submission meets <a href="https://msdlive.org/policies" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**MSD-LIVE Policies**</a> and follows best practices.

You may mark submissions as:

- **Public** — visible to everyone  
- **Restricted** — visible only to members of the associated projects  

The MSD-LIVE Data Repository is built upon the <a href="https://inveniosoftware.org/products/rdm/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**Invenio Research Data Management (RDM) platform**</a>, which is in turn based upon a refactoring of the popular <a href="https://zenodo.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**Zenodo research data repository**</a>.  

Many new RDM features are planned over the next year and, where appropriate, we will incorporate them into the MSD-LIVE Data Repository as soon as they are released.
