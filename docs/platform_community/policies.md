By using this system, the user consents to such interception, monitoring, recording, copying, auditing, inspection, and disclosure at the discretion of authorized site or Department of Energy personnel. By continuing to use this system you indicate your awareness of and consent to these terms and conditions of use. LOG OFF IMMEDIATELY if you do not agree to the conditions stated in this warning.

## Data Repository Policies

- Anyone can register for a user account and download data from MSD-LIVE
- To upload data or create new datasets, users must be part of an approved MSD-LIVE project
- Dataset authors retain full control over their data and can set appropriate licenses
- All datasets must include appropriate metadata and documentation
- MSD-LIVE reserves the right to remove content that violates policies or applicable laws

## Acceptable Use

Users of MSD-LIVE computational resources must:

- Use resources only for approved research purposes
- Not attempt unauthorized access to systems or data
- Respect intellectual property rights and data licenses
- Report security incidents or policy violations promptly
- Follow all applicable laws, regulations, and institutional policies

## Data Privacy

- MSD-LIVE does not collect personal data beyond what is necessary for account management
- User activity may be logged for security and system administration purposes
- Dataset downloads and usage may be tracked for analytics and reporting
- Users are responsible for ensuring their datasets comply with relevant data protection regulations
