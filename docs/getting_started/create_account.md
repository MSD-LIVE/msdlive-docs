# Create an Account

To get started with MSD-LIVE, create an account on the <a href="https://msdlive.org/sign-up" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**Sign Up**</a> page.

## Choose an Access Tier

During registration, you will need to select an **access tier** based on your intended use.

- **Basic tiers** provide access to MSD-LIVE data and standard features  
- **Advanced tiers** provide additional compute resources, including access to more powerful **Jupyter Notebook environments**

Select the tier that best fits your needs. If your requirements change, you can request a different tier later.

## Submit Your Request

After completing the form:

- Your request will be reviewed by an administrator  
- You will be notified once your account has been **approved**  
- Access to higher-tier resources is only available after approval  

## After Approval

Once approved, you can sign in and begin using MSD-LIVE based on your assigned access level.

## Forgot Your Password?

If you can't remember your password or aren't sure whether you have an account:

- Go to the <a href="https://msdlive.org/forgot-password" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**Forgot Password**</a> page  
- Enter your email address  

If you have an account, you will receive a password reset link.  
If you do not have an account, you will be directed to the Sign Up page.
